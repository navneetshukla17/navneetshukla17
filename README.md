## 🗂️ Product Work

> These aren't just engineering projects. Each one started with a user problem, went through discovery, and shipped as a product decision.

### 🏗️ Origin Platform — Internal Tooling Consolidation @ CashFlo
**Role:** End-to-end product ownership (discovery → delivery → rollout)

The sales and operations teams were switching between Retool, Frejun, and Interakt daily — three tools, constant context-switching, zero unified view. I mapped their workflows directly, identified the friction, wrote the requirements, and led delivery of **Origin** — a unified internal platform consolidating all three tools.

- 📋 Conducted user research with Sales (Hunters & Farmers), Ops (Vendor Finance & Supply Chain), and Admin teams
- 💰 Replaced Retool Enterprise license entirely — significant cost savings, zero third-party dependency
- 🔄 Ran UAT cycles, took structured feedback post-launch, shipped follow-on features based on real usage

---

### 🤖 BRE Agent — AI Business Rule Engine (MVP) @ CashFlo
**Role:** Problem identification → architecture → working prototype

Every policy update required manual engineering effort to reconfigure the rule engine — a bottleneck nobody had named yet. I named it, defined the solution, and built it.

- 📄 Ingests policy documents (PDF, Word, image) → auto-extracts and classifies rules
- ⚙️ Converts rules into GoRules Decision Graph format, auto-configures the engine
- ✅ Classifies invoices (PO, non-PO, GRN, non-GRN) as Approve / Hold / Reject — zero manual setup
- 🛠️ Built with: `LangChain` `LangGraph` `FastAPI` `Python` `GoRules`

---

### 📞 AI Call Summary — Build vs. Buy Decision @ CashFlo
**Role:** Product decision → build → ship

Frejun's native AI summary feature was exceeding budget at ~55 calls/day. I identified we had Gemini capacity in-house, made the call to build instead of buy, and shipped it at zero marginal cost.

- 🎯 Surfaces: caller/callee tone · customer sentiment · action items · full transcripts
- 💡 **PM decision:** build-vs-buy — replaced paid external feature with in-house Gemini Flash system
- 📊 Outcome: same capability, zero marginal cost per call
